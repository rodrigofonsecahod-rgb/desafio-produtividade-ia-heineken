## 📌 About This Challenge

This creative challenge is part of the **HEINEKEN Bootcamp — Artificial Intelligence Applied to Sales** at [DIO](https://dio.me). The goal is to build a **complete AI prompt** capable of guiding an AI to produce ready-to-use sales arguments for real field situations.

The chosen context is the **FYS** brand, a Heineken Group sparkling beverage with 50% less sugar, a humorous, sharp, and authentic tone of voice — and a major challenge of penetrating Brazil's bakery channel.

---

## 🧱 Step-by-Step Construction

### Step 1 — Define the Intent

> I want the AI to generate **ready-to-use sales arguments** for **FYS field sales representatives**, with the goal of **increasing conversion at bakeries that do not yet carry the product**.

---

### Step 2 — Add Context and Constraints

> Consider the following context: FYS is a sparkling beverage from the Heineken Group with 50% less sugar, a positioning different from leading brands, and a humorous, sharp, and completely honest tone of voice. The brand has less than 1% penetration in Brazil's bakery channel, even though bakeries are the country's top point of sale for soft drinks. Sales reps typically have very little time per approach and face recurring objections such as "I've never heard of this brand," "my customers only buy Coke or Guaraná," and "I already have enough soft drinks."
>
> The content must follow a **sales argument card format** — the bakery owner's objection highlighted, followed by the sales rep's response in no more than 3 lines. Avoid corporate language, sales clichés like "amazing product" or "unique opportunity," exaggerated profit promises, and anything that sounds like a supermarket flyer.

---

## 🚀 Final Prompt

```
You are a commercial argumentation consultant specializing in challenger brands.
Your mission is to help FYS field sales reps — FYS is a Heineken Group sparkling
beverage with 50% less sugar and a direct, slightly sharp, no-nonsense tone of voice —
convert bakery owners who don't yet carry the product.

BUSINESS CONTEXT:
- FYS has less than 1% penetration in Brazil's bakery channel
- Bakeries are the #1 soft drink retail channel in the country
- Sales reps typically have only 1 to 3 minutes per approach
- Competition (Coca-Cola, Guaraná Antarctica) is already established and well-known
- FYS doesn't compete on volume — it competes for the space of those who want something different

MISSION:
Generate 6 sales argument cards, one for each objection below. Each card must include:
1. THE OBJECTION: the exact phrase the bakery owner typically says
2. THE SALES REP'S RESPONSE: maximum 3 lines, direct and slightly witty tone aligned with FYS brand voice

OBJECTIONS TO COVER:
1. "I've never heard of this brand."
2. "My customers only ask for Coke or Guaraná."
3. "I already have way too many soft drinks in my fridge."
4. "I don't have money to test a new product right now."
5. "Low-sugar drinks don't sell in bakeries."
6. "Let me think about it and I'll get back to you."

TONE RULES:
- Direct, no fluff — just like the FYS brand speaks
- Slightly witty, but never forced or cheesy
- Never use: "excellent choice," "unique opportunity," "premium product," "amazing taste"
- The response should sound like a confident sales rep, not a brochure
```

## 💡 Why This Prompt Works

| Element | What It Does Well |
|---|---|
| **Clear intent** | Defines exactly who uses it, for whom, and what the goal is |
| **Rich context** | Real market data + brand positioning + field sales reality |
| **Defined format** | Argument card = structured, immediately usable output |
| **Specified tone** | Aligns the AI's response to the brand DNA without guesswork |
| **Objection list** | Directs the AI toward the most common real-world sales situations |
| **Explicit constraints** | Eliminates the most common AI mistakes: generic language and corporate clichés |

## 🌍 About FYS Brand

FYS is a sparkling beverage from the Heineken Group, launched in 2019 and relaunched in 2021. Key facts:

- **50% less sugar** (or zero sugar options)
- **Individual consumption** format
- **Brand voice:** humorous, sharp, and authentic — the brand jokes about not being #1
- **Main challenge:** bakeries are the largest soft drink retail channel in Brazil, yet FYS captures less than 1% of that market
- **Why?** Heineken's sales force prioritizes bars and clubs (higher beer volume), leaving bakeries underserved
