# Creative Challenge: Accelerating Your Personal Productivity with AI

**Heineken Bootcamp - Artificial Intelligence Applied to Sales | DIO**

---

## About the Challenge

This challenge aims to build a **clear and effective prompt** to guide an AI in producing useful content for personal productivity. The prompt was created in 3 steps: defining intention, adding context and constraints, and merging everything into one final command.

---

## Step-by-Step Construction

### Step 1 - Define the Intention

**What should the AI generate? For whom? What is the expected outcome?**

I want the AI to generate a weekly productivity plan for sales professionals, with the goal of increasing focus, reducing procrastination, and better organizing daily tasks.

---

### Step 2 - Add Context and Constraints

**What style do you want? Is there a size limit? What should be avoided?**

Consider the following context: a sales professional with a packed schedule, weekly targets to hit, and difficulty prioritizing urgent versus important tasks. The content should be formatted as a table or structured list organized by day of the week. Avoid technical language - use simple and direct wording.

---

### Step 3 - Final Unified Prompt

**All elements combined into one clear command:**

I want the AI to generate a weekly productivity plan for sales professionals, with the goal of increasing focus, reducing procrastination, and better organizing daily tasks. Consider the following context: a sales professional with a packed schedule, weekly targets to hit, and difficulty prioritizing urgent versus important tasks. The content should be formatted as a table or structured list organized by day of the week (Monday to Friday). Avoid technical language - use simple, motivating, and direct language suited for a results-driven salesperson.

---

## Why Does This Prompt Work?

- **Clear intention**: specifies exactly what should be generated (weekly productivity plan)
- **Defined audience**: sales professionals with specific characteristics
- **Measurable goal**: focus, reduced procrastination, and better organization
- **Rich context**: describes the professional's reality (packed schedule, targets, challenges)
- **Defined format**: table or list organized by day of the week
- **Clear constraints**: simple, motivating language without technical jargon

---

## Key Takeaway

A great prompt is built on **clear intention, context, and specific instructions**. The more relevant details you provide to the AI, the more precise and useful the generated response will be. This exercise demonstrates that well-structured prompts are the key to unlocking the full potential of generative AI tools.
